## Editorial rule

Every public case study separates a documented problem from the author's original motive. Where the latter is missing, the site says so. An alternative that already works is named plainly. No performance, operational, accuracy or playable-release claim is derived from a diagram, static test or synthetic browser interaction.

The alternative/source URLs and dated comparison are in [PROJECT-ALTERNATIVES-RESEARCH-2026-11-16.md](PROJECT-ALTERNATIVES-RESEARCH-2026-11-16.md). The nine project-specific Codex chats hold the detailed source dossiers and project-specific visual directions.
